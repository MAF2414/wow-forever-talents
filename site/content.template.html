18 new Tier 1 class/role sets: six member IDs each, with bonuses at 2, 3, 4 and 5 pieces. “Tier 1” comes from the internal bonus spell names.

The 108 referenced tier items have no ItemSparse metadata in this snapshot. Item stats, drop sources and raid assignments remain unverified.

Raid announcements and client records have different coverage. Blizzard announced Hyjal Summit (20 players) and Barrow Deeps (10 players). No newly added map is typed as a raid in the inspected build, and no boss roster for these raids was established.

Hyjal Crater is an arena-type record. It does not verify Hyjal Summit. Empty journal tables and partial NPC data also limit what this search can establish.
