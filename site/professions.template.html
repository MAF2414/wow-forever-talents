Comparison guide & sources

Classic: Era 1.14.4.51395. SoD: 1.15.9.69722. SoD categories have a separate filter.

Recipe comparisons cover ingredients, crafted results, descriptions, cast times, cooldowns, tools, stations and skill-up thresholds. Item stat changes are separate from recipe changes.

Book skill is the requirement printed on a linked recipe item. Skill-up thresholds are the lower and upper trivial-skill values in the recipe record; they are not trainer learning requirements.

Camping buff values follow the selected character level. Conditional XP bonuses remain marked as conditional. Food restoration totals are omitted when the client supplies a dynamic formula; original item text is available on each card.
